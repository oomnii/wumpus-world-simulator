How to Play & AI Cycle

Navigate the grid from (0,0). Avoid pits and the Wumpus. Grab gold when you see Glitter, return to start, and climb out.

Percepts

  • Breeze — pit nearby
  • Stench — Wumpus nearby
  • Glitter — gold here
  • Scream — arrow killed Wumpus
  • Bump — hit world edge

Scoring

  • Each action: −1
  • Gold: +1000
  • Arrow: −10
  • Escape with gold: +1000
  • Death: −1000

AI reasoning cycle

  1. Observe percepts
  2. Update knowledge base
  3. Infer safe / dangerous cells
  4. Decide next action
  5. Execute one action
  6. Update visualization
